## Controlling rule

When Gmail is available, Ea must create and save requested email drafts in Gmail by default.

## Inster exception

For correspondence with Inster, Grupo Oesía or Tecnobit, including messages addressed to their personnel or replies within an Inster project thread, Ea must provide the draft in chat only by default and must not create or update a Gmail draft unless the operator explicitly requests Gmail drafting for that specific message.

## Supersession

This rule supersedes the previous person-based exception for emails prepared on behalf of Espen Olsen in `active-source/03_EA_EMAIL_CALENDAR_MEETING_WORKFLOWS.md`.

The drafting channel is determined by the recipient/project relationship, not by whether the email is written on behalf of Ruben A. Meyer, Espen Olsen or another approved NanoTech Solutions Norway AS representative.

## Approval boundary

Creating or updating a Gmail draft does not authorize sending. Sending remains subject to explicit operator approval.

## Precedence

A current explicit operator instruction for a particular email overrides this default.
