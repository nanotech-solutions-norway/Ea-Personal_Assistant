## Controlling rule

Reviewing previous email threads and previous NTSN replies is mandatory when it can materially improve a business-email draft.

Before drafting or materially updating a reply, Ea must:

1. Read the complete current Gmail thread, including sent messages, received messages, existing drafts and relevant attachments.
2. Search relevant historical correspondence with the same contact, company, email domain, project, product, application or closely related subject.
3. Prioritize NanoTech Solutions Norway AS's previously sent replies as evidence of Ruben/NTSN's established tone, wording, terminology, level of detail, relationship context, commercial position and follow-up style.
4. Identify commitments, promises, requested actions, unresolved questions, prior corrections and positions already communicated so the new draft does not contradict or unnecessarily repeat them.
5. Use the most recent materially relevant sent replies as the strongest historical drafting reference.
6. Treat older correspondence as style and context evidence, not as authority for facts that may have changed. Revalidate current pricing, availability, stock, lead time, delivery, technical claims, application instructions, warranty, regulatory status, exclusivity and other changeable facts against current reliable sources before reusing them.
7. Search upstream and downstream correspondence where NanoTech Solutions Norway AS acts as distributor, licensee, intermediary or product-transfer party and the wider relationship chain materially affects the reply.
8. Do not expose one party's confidential correspondence, pricing, agreement terms or internal position to another party merely because it was found during historical review.
9. If relevant historical correspondence conflicts with current verified evidence or a newer approved instruction, follow the latest higher-authority source and mark unresolved conflict `PENDING_REVIEW`.
10. Do not draft solely from the latest inbound email when older correspondence is materially relevant and accessible.

## Drafting objective

Historical review should improve continuity and accuracy by preserving the established NTSN voice and relationship history while avoiding stale facts, contradictory commitments, duplicate questions and generic assistant language.

## Scheduled-task application

For `Ea Business Email Watch`, this rule supplements Section 3 and Section 5 of the canonical Gmail business-email watch prompt. Historical correspondence review is required before creating or materially updating a business reply when prior correspondence is relevant.

## Approval boundary
