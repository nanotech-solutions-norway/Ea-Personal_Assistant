## Key visual controls

- A4 portrait, one intended page.
- Libre Baskerville family for all visible text.
- 18 pt Bold title; 12 pt Bold section headings; ~9.96 pt body; ~8.04 pt compact metadata/footer/signature labels.
- Canonical logo appears only at top right.
- No default bottom-right logo.
- No Address/Website QR graphics in the 05C final PDF unless explicitly requested.
- Thin grey divider below every Terms and conditions row, including Lead time.
- Grey-header Items table: Description / Unit / Qnt. / Price / Sub total.
- Currency symbol displayed in monetary values.
- Signature/stamp approximately 45 mm wide and handwritten baseline directly on signature line.
- Note block lower left; total and signature blocks lower right with no overlap.
